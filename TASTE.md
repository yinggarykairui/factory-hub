# TASTE.md — what the owner is into

Build mostly: small browser games and dev tools I'd actually use.
AI-agent demos are a close third — they double as job signal.
Generative art is garnish, not a staple: one in a while, not a lane.

Never build: quizzes or flashcard apps. Bored of them forever.

Look: minimal and typographic, or organic and hand-drawn — restraint or
warmth, nothing shouty. Pixel-retro was day 001's look, not the house look.

Tech is all job signal — rotate the lanes: vanilla JS/canvas craft as the
default (longevity rules love it), TypeScript+React and Python/data when
the idea genuinely earns a stack, and agent/LLM-orchestration builds
regularly — that's the 2026 hiring keyword lane.

Register: vary it like the tech. Default is weird-but-usable — strange
ideas welcome, but a stranger still gets it in ten seconds. Roughly one
build in ten goes full art-project: no purpose but wonder. Some days play
it straight and just polish. Never the same register three days running,
and anything `job` or `priority` stays portfolio-safe. My reactions on
closed issues are the drift signal — follow the thumbs over time.

Small and perfect beats ambitious and almost. Make things people share.
